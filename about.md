## 1. Positioning

VERL Systems is a digital systems company. We design, build and maintain the websites, automations and AI systems that businesses run on.

The site presents **the company**, not a single offer. It never narrows VERL to one service, one sector or one type of client. A visitor should leave thinking: *"This is a serious company that builds serious systems."*

**One-liner**
- **EN:** VERL Systems designs and builds the digital systems businesses run on.
- **TR [draft]:** VERL Systems, işletmelerin üzerinde çalıştığı dijital sistemleri tasarlar ve kurar.

**Tagline:** Systems. Built to run.
**TR [draft]:** Çalışmak için kurulan sistemler.

## 3. What we build — three capabilities

### 01 — Web Platforms
Company websites, product and campaign pages, and web applications. Fast, precise, built to turn visitors into clients.
**TR [draft]:** Kurumsal web siteleri, kampanya sayfaları ve web uygulamaları. Hızlı, özenli ve ziyaretçiyi müşteriye dönüştürmek için tasarlanmış.

### 02 — Automation & Integrations
We connect the tools a business already uses — WhatsApp, CRM, calendars, spreadsheets, payment and booking tools — and remove the manual work between them.
**TR [draft]:** İşletmenizin zaten kullandığı araçları birbirine bağlıyor, aradaki elle yapılan işi ortadan kaldırıyoruz.

### 03 — AI Systems
Assistants and agents that answer, qualify, book and report — trained on the business's own information and connected to its real tools.
**TR [draft]:** İşletmenin kendi bilgileriyle çalışan, soruları yanıtlayan, talepleri ayıklayan ve randevu alan yapay zekâ asistanları.

**Rule:** the three capabilities are presented as one integrated offer — a VERL system can combine all three. Never present VERL as "a website agency" or "a WhatsApp bot company".

## 5. How we work

| Phase | What happens | What the client receives |
|---|---|---|
| 01 — Discover | We study the business, its tools and where time and clients are lost. | A short discovery summary |
| 02 — Blueprint | We design the system before building it. | A system blueprint (flow diagram + scope + timeline) |
| 03 — Build | We build, test with real scenarios, and launch. | The working system + documentation |
| 04 — Run | We monitor, support and improve after launch. | Post-launch support period |

TR phase names [draft]: Keşif · Plan · Kurulum · Sürdürme

## 10. Voice

- **Company voice:** calm, confident, precise. Speaks about outcomes (time saved, clients served, work removed), not about features or tools.
- Short sentences. No exclamation marks. No hype.
- **Technical depth** appears inside case studies and capability pages under "How we build it / Nasıl kuruyoruz".
- Turkish: formal `siz`, natural, never translated-sounding.
- Studio speaks as "biz / we". The founder appears in the third person on company pages.
- Banned words: see `CLAUDE.md` §7.
